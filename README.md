## About Me

I'm a **Backend / AI Engineer** with nearly two years of experience building scalable backend systems and LLM-powered applications in production.

Day to day I design and ship **REST APIs** with **FastAPI** and **Django**, model data in **PostgreSQL**, and run async work through **Celery**, **Redis**, and **Azure Service Bus**. I deploy and operate services on **Microsoft Azure** (Container Apps, Blob Storage) with **Docker** and **GitHub Actions**.

On the AI side I build **LLM, RAG, and multi-agent systems** with **LangGraph**, **MCP**, **OpenAI**, and **Azure OpenAI** — chatbots, knowledge retrieval, function calling, and natural-language querying over structured data.

**What I work on:**

- **Domain monitoring & site-block detection** — scan pipelines, per-ISP network checks, lifecycle reconciliation, and dashboard reporting
- **Live sports copyright workflows** — match operations, staff assignment, domain delivery, violation-report exports, and audit trails
- **Platform backend** — authorization (RBAC/ABAC), WebSocket and Redis Pub/Sub, file imports, Gmail integration, and integration testing
- **LLM products** — RAG pipelines, multi-agent orchestration, knowledge graphs, and MCP-based tool routing

Currently at **PHANLAW**, on a production platform used for domain monitoring and live sports copyright operations. Previously at **AIAIVN**, building backend services and AI chatbot workflows for LLM/RAG applications.
